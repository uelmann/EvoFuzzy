## Plain-language conclusion

Mostly no — discrimination lives on the losing tail. The engine is labeled LONG-SIDE GAP: it does not jointly clear a significant TOP spread and a symmetry ratio ≥ 0.4 on two universes at either horizon. Bucket curves at h=7 top-40 have Spearman ρ=1.000 (rank vs mean residual). TOP spread there is 0.0057 (NW-t 2.98) vs BOTTOM 0.0098 (t 4.01), ratio 0.577. Against the fair EW top-40 basket, COMBO-LO-H alpha is 0.051 (NW-t 0.46) and COMBO-LO-U alpha is -0.008 (t -0.07). Reference long-pick residual vs the same-date CS mean is -0.0015 (Sleeve A, 467 days with longs) and +0.0033 (Sleeve B, 1438 days). The model ranks alts, but the winner-side gap vs the loser-side is the classification object. Neither finding changes the reference book.
